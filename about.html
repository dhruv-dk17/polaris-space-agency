About Polaris

Pioneering Aerospace from Indian Soil

Science-driven space agency founded in 2018 in Bengaluru, operating rocket launches, planetary missions, and a Ladakh observatory.

Our Philosophy

Science First. Always.

Polaris was built on a single premise: that scientific knowledge and engineering precision can overcome the harshest frontiers. We design cryogenic propulsion systems, rover robotics, and precision orbital guidance in India.

Facilities

Strategic Operations Across India

Sriharikota, AP

Satish Dhawan Complex

Primary rocket launch center equipped for polar, equatorial, and geostationary orbital delivery missions.

Bengaluru, Karnataka

Engineering HQ

Headquarters for vehicle design, payload integration, trajectory simulations, and mission control.

Hanle, Ladakh

Dark Sky Observatory

High-altitude optical telescope array operating at 4,500m with zero light pollution for deep space sky surveys.

History

Eight Years of Exploration

From a Bengaluru startup to India's most ambitious space exploration program.

2025 · ARES-1 Mars Lander

Robotic Mars explorer deployed, analyzing mineralogy and storm cycles on the Martian surface.

2024 · LUNA-2 Moon South Pole

Second lunar rover mapped shadowed craters and discovered subsurface ice signatures.

2023 · ZEUS-1 Jupiter Orbiter

Entered Jovian orbit after 5-year transit, surveying radiation belts and storm physics.

2022 · LUNA-1 First Lunar Rover

First rover soft landing on lunar regolith testing thermal baseline systems.

2020 · First Commercial Satellite

Delivered communications payload to geostationary orbit using PSLV-Heavy.

2018 · Agency Founded

Polaris incorporated in Bengaluru to advance reusable cryogenic launch technology.
