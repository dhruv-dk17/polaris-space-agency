Scientific Research

Pushing Boundaries of Space Knowledge

From cryogenic propulsion engineering to dark-sky telescope observations in Ladakh.

Dark Sky Observatory · Hanle, Ladakh · 4,500m

Polaris Massive Telescope Array

Operating at 4,500 meters altitude with zero light pollution, our precision telescope scans deep sky targets: distant emission nebulae, galactic clusters, solar chromospheric flares, and orbital mechanics.

Active Domains

Research Programs

Life Sciences

Astrobiology & Regolith

Analyzing spectroscopic data from lunar south pole soil samples and ice crystal mechanics.

Propulsion

Cryogenic Propulsion

Designing high-expansion LOX/LH2 upper stages targeting full reusability by 2028.

Earth Sciences

Earth Ingress Imaging

Developing multi-spectral payloads for weather telemetry and glacier change mapping.

Astrophysics

Nebula & Galaxy Surveys

Mapping spectral lines of H-alpha and OIII nebulae contributing to global open catalogs.

Heliophysics

Solar Wind & Flares

Monitoring solar eruptions, coronal ejections, and magnetic polarity shifts.

Planetary Science

Mars Atmosphere Models

ARES-1 telemetry powers predictive dust density models for future crewed habitats.
