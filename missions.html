Missions Registry

Every Mission. Every Frontier.

From lunar south pole surveys to Jupiter atmospheric probes — exploring the solar system from Indian soil.

Lunar · 2022

LUNA-1 First Lunar Rover

Mapped geological strata and thermal cycles in permanent shadow regions of the lunar south pole.
Lunar · 2024

LUNA-2 Water Ice Survey

Deep spectrometry at the south pole confirmed subsurface moisture traces in shadowed craters.
Mars · 2025

ARES-1 Red Explorer

Active robotic rover tracking atmospheric dust storms and seismic vibrations across the equatorial plains.
Deep Space · 2023

ZEUS-1 Jupiter Orbiter

Inserted into Jovian orbit to analyze radiation belts and Great Red Spot cloud physics.
Satellite · 2026

ASTRA-7 Telecom Payload

High-bandwidth telecommunication satellite inserted into geostationary orbit using PSLV-Heavy.
Satellite · 2026

NaviGlobe GPS Payload

Medium Earth orbit satellite expanding India's regional positioning constellation network.
